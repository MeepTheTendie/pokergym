Your data

Privacy and your data

Free browser demo · Updated October 17, 2026

Play Poker Gym lets you practice without signing up. The demo has no checkout and no payment collection. Your decisions are evaluated on your device; they are not sent to a training server or AI service. We do collect product analytics and session replays so we can see how the game is used and improve it — see Analytics and session replay. Your saved progress still lives in this browser and is never uploaded.

What stays in your browser

The game saves your chosen class, experience, badges, lessons, practice accuracy, recent sessions and mistake notebook in this browser’s local storage. Session dates and review/retry counts are part of that save. Your sound preference is saved separately. The weekly sample guide stores its completed first and latest retry scores and unfinished decision results in separate local records so you can resume. These guide scores can be removed with the same site-data controls. These records are not linked to a cloud account or synced between devices.

The live game also downloads game files into a browser cache for offline practice. The weekly sample offers an explicit download button that caches its page and scripts together with the free practice game. Email and GitHub feedback links need a connection. Saves and cached files remain until you or your browser remove them. Storage may be unavailable in private browsing or embedded games; the game displays a warning when it cannot save progress.

Keep or remove your progress

  1. To keep a copy, open Back up / restore progress in the game and choose Prepare backup. Copy the text into a private file or note. This does not upload it. A backup includes your profile, records and mistake notebook; avoid posting it publicly.
  2. To remove this browser’s saved progress, sound preference and offline download, close Play Poker Gym tabs and use your browser’s site-data controls to clear data for the address where you played. For the live demo, that is playpokergym.com (or pokergym.history-atlas.workers.dev if you played at the older address; each address keeps its own separate save). Browser menus differ; look for site settings, storage or site data. Clearing data removes your local save and offline access. Reopening online can download the game again.
  3. Delete any backup copies separately if you no longer want them. Clearing browser data cannot delete a copy you saved elsewhere. Removing a home-screen shortcut alone may leave browser data behind.

On another host or in an embedded portable demo, data belongs to that host’s browser storage. Use that host’s site-data controls. The game cannot recover cleared progress without a backup you kept.

Feedback is your choice

The feedback form prepares an email draft on your device. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send it, the recipient receives the answers and note you reviewed, along with your email address and any information your email service includes. The feedback recipient is meep@mail.grokbot.com. Use that address for questions about feedback you sent or to request its removal; do not include a save backup or sensitive details.

GitHub feedback is optional, public and requires a GitHub account. Preparing feedback sends nothing. Opening the GitHub draft sends your reviewed answers and note to GitHub in the link, which may remain in browser history; you must submit it yourself to publish a new issue. The game feedback draft includes no player profile, scores or save backup. The weekly sample feedback draft includes completed first/latest aggregate scores when available, plus your optional comment; you can edit or remove any part before opening a draft. Its feedback answers and comment are not saved by the page. You can also copy the draft into the existing feedback thread. GitHub handles account and posted information under its own policies. Use GitHub’s controls to manage your public posts. The game’s invitation text contains only the public demo link and description, without your scores or profile.

Reminders (web notifications)

On the end-of-run recap you can ask for one reminder a day. Choosing it asks your browser for notification permission; nothing is sent unless you allow it. If you allow it, your browser gives us a push address and two public encryption keys, which are stored on our server (Cloudflare KV) so the reminder can be delivered. No name, email, scores, or save data are stored with them, and they are not shared. A reminder opens the free daily drill. Choosing “Reminders on · tap to stop” unsubscribes on that device and deletes the stored address; denying permission or clearing site data leaves nothing behind. This is separate from the analytics described below.

Analytics and session replay

To understand how the game is used and improve it, the live demo loads PostHog, a product-analytics service. It records the pages you view, the buttons and hands you play and general session information, tied to a random identifier kept in this browser (a first-party cookie and local storage). It also records session replays: an anonymised reconstruction of on-screen activity such as taps and screen flow, so we can see where players get stuck. Every text field — including any feedback note or backup text you type or paste — is masked in replays and is never recorded.

Analytics is loaded through our own domain and processed by PostHog on servers in the United States. It is not used for advertising and is not sold or shared with advertisers. See PostHog’s privacy policy. To stop analytics, clear this site’s data and cookies (this resets the identifier) or use your browser’s tracking-protection or content-blocking settings; the game keeps working normally without it.

Online hosting and external links

The live demo is hosted by Cloudflare. Loading it online sends ordinary web requests, including your IP address and requested URLs, to the hosting service. Hosting may process technical request information for delivery, security and diagnostics. Local progress is not included in those requests. The visit counter itself stores only the date, the page (landing, game or published learning guide) and a link label from a fixed list; it stores no IP address, user agent or cookie. Product analytics and session replay are described above and are handled separately by PostHog.

Links to GitHub and email open services outside Play Poker Gym. Their handling of your information is separate from the game. A portable copy may be hosted by someone else with different hosting practices.

This describes the current free demo. There are no paid subscriptions. Accounts or cloud sync would require updated disclosures before launch. It does not establish app-store approval.
