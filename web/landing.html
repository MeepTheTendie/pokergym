What you’re playing today

This is a free browser trainer that plays on desktop, tablet and phone. It keeps working offline once you have loaded it. Progress is stored locally in your browser—not in a cloud account. Clearing browser data can remove your save. This demo has no checkout or real-money prizes. Learning the best play lowers the house edge; it never beats it.
