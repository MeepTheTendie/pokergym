Good to know

Is this gambling?
No. There is no real money, no betting and no prizes. It teaches the rules and the best decisions. Learning the best play lowers the house edge in casino games; it doesn’t guarantee a win.
Is it really free?
Yes. No signup, no ads, no purchases. If it helps you, there’s an optional Ko-fi tip link below — tips unlock nothing.
Do I need an account or app?
No. It runs in your browser on desktop, tablet and phone, and keeps working offline once loaded. There’s no app-store release yet.
Where is my progress saved?
Locally in your browser, not in a cloud account. Clearing browser data can remove your save, so use the in-app backup.
Does it teach full poker strategy?
Hold’em practice covers hand reading — which five cards count — not betting or bluffing.
What do you track?
No ads and no third-party trackers. The host keeps anonymous daily page-visit counts. Privacy details.
