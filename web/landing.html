Questions

Is Play Poker Gym free?

Yes. Every game, the Daily Drill, mistake review and progress saving are free. There is no signup, subscription or paid tier.

Does Play Poker Gym use real money?

No. There are no deposits, bets, cash prizes or real-money wagering. The cards and odds are simulated for learning.

Which card games does it teach?

Blackjack, Jacks or Better video poker, baccarat and Texas Hold’em hand reading. Each five-hand session is coached, and missed decisions return on a spaced schedule.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is saved in your browser on this device. You can export a backup before clearing site data or changing devices.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after the first load. Add it to your home screen to open it like an app.

Is the coaching the same as a casino strategy chart?

No. The coach compares average outcomes under the simulator’s rules. Different rules or a depleted shoe can change a decision; the guides note the assumptions.
