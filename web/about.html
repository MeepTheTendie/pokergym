About Play Poker Gym

Updated November 25, 2026

Play Poker Gym (playpokergym.com) is a free browser trainer for four casino card games: blackjack, Jacks or Better video poker, baccarat and Texas Hold’em hand reading. You play short five-hand sessions, and a coach grades each decision and explains why. Missed decisions come back for review on a spaced schedule, and a shared Daily Drill deals five hands each day. The strategy guides answer one decision per page and link to a drill for that exact hand.

How the answers are computed

Every grade the trainer gives is computed in your browser by the same code that runs the game. The guides state the answer that code reaches under the rules listed in the next section.

  • Blackjack. The coach calculates the exact expected value of each legal action (hit, stand, double and split) by working through every card still left in the shoe, weighted by how many of each rank remain. It uses no lookup table and no random sampling. Because the round ends at once when the dealer has a natural, the coach only compares actions in hands where the dealer does not have blackjack. Split hands use one documented simplification that moves the expected value only in the fourth decimal place. The strategy charts on the guides are checked against this engine and against standard six-deck basic strategy for these rules.
  • Video poker. For each deal the coach tries all 32 ways to hold or discard the five cards and averages the payout over every possible draw from the 47 unseen cards. The hold with the highest expected return is the answer. The guides use the full-pay 9/6 Jacks or Better pay table; the trainer can also grade Bonus Poker, Double Double Bonus and Deuces Wild against their own pay tables.
  • Baccarat. Baccarat odds come from an exact walk through every possible deal from an eight-deck shoe under the standard third-card rules, not from simulation. A baccarat player makes no drawing decisions, so the trainer quizzes you on the odds and on which bet carries the lowest house edge, using these exact figures.
  • Texas Hold’em. The trainer asks you to pick the best five cards from the seven you can see and accepts any five that make a hand of equal strength. Drawing odds on the Hold’em guides count your outs among the cards you cannot see (47 after the flop, 46 after the turn), using only the visible cards. Play Poker Gym does not teach a complete betting or bluffing strategy.

Rules the answers assume

GameRules
BlackjackSix decks. Dealer stands on all 17s (S17). Blackjack pays 3:2. Double on any first two cards, double after split allowed (DAS). No resplitting aces, no surrender, up to four hands after splits.
Video pokerJacks or Better, 9/6 pay table (full house pays 9, flush pays 6), one 52-card deck per deal.
BaccaratEight decks. Standard third-card rules. Banker wins pay 1:1 less a 5% commission, Player pays 1:1, Tie pays 8:1, and a tie pushes Player and Banker bets.
Texas Hold’emBest five cards out of seven (two hole cards and five on the board). The ace can play high or low in a straight.

Different table rules can change some answers, for example a dealer who hits soft 17, fewer decks, surrender, or a lower video poker pay table. Guides note this where it matters.

Free, with no signup

The trainer and every guide are free to use in a web browser. There is no account to create and nothing to download, and progress is saved in your own browser. See the privacy page for what is stored and what analytics collects.
