## Fun check

The new home page is a real improvement. The first thing I see is a hand ("Dealer shows 10. You have 16. Your move."), one tap plays it, and the coach's answer to my deliberately wrong Stand ("Hit. By a hair. Hitting loses 53.5¢ per dollar, standing 53.7¢") is the best coaching line on the site. It's surprising, specific, and makes you want the next hand. The drill flowed: about three minutes, clean "Your call" states, five green squares, and Challenge a friend right next to Replay. The low point came right after that. In practice, when I got one wrong, the game said the better play was different, then said my hold "was still the right play", and never told me what to hold. That breaks the whole promise of a coach (#1). Ask coach saying "Keep 2 cards" without naming them is the same problem (#3). What would bring me back tomorrow: the streak, plus coaching as concrete as that home-page line on every miss. Fix #1–#3 first; they are small and they protect the core loop.
