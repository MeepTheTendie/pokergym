## Fun check

The landing hand is still the hook. On desktop it's one tap, a "Not quite", and a line that tells you two numbers you didn't know. The drill felt quick and fair: two Hold'em reads, one blackjack, a baccarat commission question and a video-poker hold, about two minutes, five green squares and a countdown to #325. The Hold'em "find your best five" hands were the dullest part this time. Both were plain high-card boards, and a correct answer earns only "Your best five is High Card." Practice blackjack was the opposite: when I missed, the coach told me what to do and not why, so a miss felt like a fine rather than a lesson (#5). On phone, the moment it stops being fun is concrete. After the first drill hand you're staring at four greyed-out buttons with "Next hand" hidden underneath (#1), and four hands later the cards you're supposed to tap are scrolled off the top (#4). What would bring me back tomorrow: the streak and the #325 countdown already ask for it. Fix #1 and #2 so phone players get there, and make every miss carry the home page's "this one costs X, that one costs Y" line.
