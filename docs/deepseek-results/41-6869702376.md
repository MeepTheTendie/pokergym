## What changed (by item)

1. **Wrong-play coaching (High, report #1).**
   - `web/app.js` `renderStatus()`: a non-paying draw now branches on
     `round.holdBest` — "The hold was still the right play." vs "Your hold was
     not the best play."
   - The coach line on a miss now shows `review.correction` inline, in both
     practice (`settleHand`) and the Daily (`settleDailyHand`), via a new
     `coachReason(review)` helper. The dangling "The review explains the better
     play." / "here is why." / "the review explains why." promises are removed.
   - `renderSessionSummary()` now lists **every** miss (`sessionMistakes`), not
     just `sessionMistakes[0]`.

2. **Coach card skips the result (Medium, #4).** Removed `advanceCoachDialogue`
   and the `#masterBox` click/keydown handlers, dropped the misleading `▼`
   (`#masterPrompt`) from `web/index.html`, and removed the now-bogus
   `role="button"`/`tabindex` from the card. "Next hand" is the one continue.

3. **Coach line glue (Low, #5).** New `endSentence()` helper closes a bare
   label (blackjack's `outcomeLabel` returns "You lose") before the verdict is
   joined, in `settleHand`, `settleDailyHand` and the daily coached branch. The
   Daily verdict now reads "Next: hand 2 of 5" instead of "hand 2 of 5".

4. **Coached hand points at a closed panel (Low, #6).** `settleHand` no longer
   hides `coachPanel`/`why` for a coached hand, so "Read the explanation" has
   the coach's hold to read; the next hand still closes it.

5. **Practice header lags (Low, #7).** Extracted `renderBattleLabel()` (used at
   deal time and now re-run in `settleHand`), so the header tracks the settled
   hand count.

6. **Coach tip ALL CAPS (Low, #8).** `src/rpg/lessons.js` titles/speakers/text
   converted to title/sentence case.

7. **Correct plays get no why in BJ/VP (Low, #9).** New `correctPlayWhy()`
   appends `reasonFor()` (VP) or the best-action EV (blackjack) to "Good read."

8. **Layout shift on first VP hold (Low, #10).** `#holdHint` now reserves its
   height in video poker (toggled via `visibility`, not `hidden`) so Draw does
   not move when the one-time hint appears/leaves; non-VP games still collapse
   it.
