Gutshot straight draw: odds you hit by the river?

The answer

About 16.5% by the river — four outs with two cards to come.

Practice five Hold’em hands →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

A gutshot fills only through one rank, so it has four outs rather than eight. That halves the open-ended number.

On the turn it is about 8.7%, which is why chasing a gutshot to a single bet is usually a losing habit.

Questions

Is a gutshot worth calling?

Only when the pot odds or implied odds justify four outs. On the turn that needs a big pot.

What is the 4-and-2 rule for a gutshot?

Four outs × 4 ≈ 16% on the flop, × 2 ≈ 8% on the turn.

How do I turn outs into odds quickly?

Use the rule of 4 and 2: multiply your outs by 4 on the flop (two cards to come) or by 2 on the turn. Nine flush outs is about 36% on the flop and 18% on the turn.
