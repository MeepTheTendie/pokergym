Why

Video poker is a game of one decision per hand: which cards to keep. The trainer grades that decision on every deal against the 9/6 Jacks or Better pay table, which returns 99.54% with perfect play.

Typical lessons: keep both pairs in two pair, keep a paying pair of jacks or better over four to a flush, and keep three of a kind rather than chasing a flush.

Five-hand sessions keep it short, and hands you miss are brought back on a spaced schedule until you hold them correctly.
