Soft 18 (A-7) against a 3: double or stand?

Double against a 3. Stand against a 2, 7 or 8; hit against 9, 10 or ace.

Against a weak dealer card, soft 18 is an opportunity: the ace means you cannot bust, so the extra bet costs little.

Against a 9 or better the hand is not strong enough to stand, so the same total turns into a hit.

Questions

Why is soft 18 different from hard 18?

The ace can drop to one, so hitting cannot bust you. That makes drawing and doubling far safer than with a hard total.

What if doubling is not allowed?

Stand against 2 through 8 and hit against 9, 10 and ace.
