Flush draw on the flop: odds you hit by the river?

The answer

About 35% — roughly one time in three.

Practice five Hold’em hands →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

A flush draw on the flop has 9 outs. With two cards to come the chance of at least one is 1 − (38/47 × 37/46) ≈ 35%.

Calling a bet on the flop alone uses the one-card number, about 19%. The bigger figure only applies if you expect to see both cards.

Questions

What are the odds of hitting a flush draw by the river?

About 35% with two cards to come, using 9 outs.

What are the odds on the turn alone?

About 19%, or roughly 1 in 5.2.

How do I turn outs into odds quickly?

Use the rule of 4 and 2: multiply your outs by 4 on the flop (two cards to come) or by 2 on the turn. Nine flush outs is about 36% on the flop and 18% on the turn.
