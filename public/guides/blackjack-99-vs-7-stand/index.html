9-9 against a dealer 7: split or stand?

Stand. Split 9s against 2 through 6, 8 and 9; stand against 7, 10 and ace.

Eighteen is a strong total, and a 7 is the dealer card where 18 performs best relative to two new hands starting at 9.

Splitting throws away a made 18 to chase two hands that each need work. Against a 7 the maths says keep the 18.

Questions

Do you always split nines?

No. Stand against 7, 10 and ace; split against 2–6, 8 and 9.

Why split 9s against a 9?

Because 18 pushes or loses to the dealer’s likely 19, while two hands starting on 9 have more room to improve.
