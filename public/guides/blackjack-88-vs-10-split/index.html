8-8 against a dealer 10: split or hit?

The answer

Split. Eights are the one pair you split against every dealer card.

Play this exact hand →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

Sixteen is the worst total in the game: you bust on any ten and you lose to every pat hand. Standing on 16 against a 10 loses most of the time.

Splitting turns one hopeless 16 into two hands that each start with an 8. Neither is a favourite, but both are better than the single 16 you would otherwise hold.

Questions

Do you always split eights?

Yes. Against every dealer upcard, splitting two 8s is better than hitting, standing or doubling a 16.

Should you split eights against a 10 when doubling is allowed?

Yes. The split is still correct; the alternative is standing on 16, which is the worst hand you can hold.

Which blackjack rules is this answer for?

Six decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double after split allowed, no surrender. The free trainer grades with those exact rules and shows the expected value of each option.
