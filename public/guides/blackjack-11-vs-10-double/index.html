11 against a dealer 10: double or hit?

The answer

Double, if the table allows it. Otherwise hit.

Practice five blackjack hands →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

Eleven is the best doubling total. Any ten, jack, queen or king makes 21, a 9 makes 20, and no single card can bust you.

The dealer 10 is strong, but once it has checked for blackjack you are still the favourite. For 6-5 from a full six-deck shoe, the trainer’s engine puts doubling at about +0.18 per unit bet and hitting at about +0.12, so the double earns roughly 6 cents more per dollar.

If the dealer hits soft 17, double 11 against an ace too.

Rules this answer assumes

Six decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double on any first two cards, double after split allowed, no surrender, and the dealer checks for blackjack before you act, so a dealer blackjack never takes a double or split bet. These are the rules the free trainer deals and grades with.

What changes the answer

  • Dealer hits soft 17. Double 11 against an ace as well, so 11 becomes a double against every upcard. In single- and double-deck games, 11 is a double against everything too.
  • No-hole-card games. In European-style games where the dealer takes your doubled bet when it turns over a blackjack, hit 11 against a 10 or an ace.
  • Three or more cards. An 11 like 4-3-4 can’t be doubled in most casinos (the trainer only doubles your first two cards), so hit it.
  • After a split. With double after split (the trainer’s rule), double a new 11 the same way. Without it, hit.

Questions

Do you double 11 against an ace?

It depends on one rule. In a multi-deck game where the dealer stands on soft 17 (the trainer’s rules), hit. If the dealer hits soft 17, or in single- and double-deck games, double.

Double or hit if doubles are restricted?

Hit. The decision is still profitable, just not doubled.

Do you double 11 against a 10 with no hole card?

No. In European no-hole-card games, where a dealer blackjack also takes the doubled part of your bet, hit 11 against a 10.
