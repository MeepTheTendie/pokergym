11 against a dealer 10: double or hit?

Double, if the table allows it. Otherwise hit.

Eleven is the best doubling total. Any ten, jack, queen or king makes 21, and an ace makes a soft 22 that plays as 12.

The dealer 10 is strong, which is exactly why you want the extra unit in while you still have the edge on the next card.

Questions

Do you double 11 against an ace?

In most rule sets you hit 11 against an ace, because the dealer is less likely to bust and the ace is a strong upcard.

Double or hit if doubles are restricted?

Hit. The decision is still profitable, just not doubled.
