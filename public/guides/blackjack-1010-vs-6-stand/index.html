10-10 against a 6: split or stand?

Stand. Never split tens, whatever the dealer shows.

Twenty beats every dealer total except 21, and the dealer busts often from a 6. Splitting gives that away.

The only reason to split is to improve a weak total. Twenty is not weak, so the split loses money in every count you will meet.

Questions

Is splitting tens ever correct?

Only at extreme positive counts in a counting system, and even then it is marginal. Basic strategy never splits tens.

What if the dealer shows a 6?

Stand. A 6 is the dealer’s worst card and 20 is already the winner most of the time.
