Soft 18 (A-7) against a 9: hit or stand?

Hit.

Soft 18 is not a strong 18. Against a 9, standing gives the dealer two chances to make a pat hand that beats you.

Hitting cannot bust: the ace counts as one, so you keep a total even if you draw a ten. The flexibility is the whole point of the soft total.

Questions

Do you hit soft 18 against a 9?

Yes. Basic strategy hits soft 18 against 9, 10 and ace, and stands against 2, 7 and 8.

Can you bust by hitting soft 18?

No. The ace drops to one, so the hand stays soft and cannot bust on the next card.
