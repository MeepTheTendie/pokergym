Blackjack 12 vs 2: hit or stand? (2026)

Hit. Hit 12 against a 2 or a 3; stand on 12 against 4, 5 and 6.

A dealer 2 or 3 busts less often than a 4, 5 or 6, so standing on 12 leaves you relying on a bust that usually does not come.

Only a ten-value card busts your 12. Every other card improves the hand, so hitting wins more over time even though you will sometimes bust. Our coach scores hit ahead of stand here.

Questions

Is 12 against a 2 really a hit?

Yes. Standard basic strategy hits hard 12 against a 2 and a 3, and stands against 4 through 6. The edge is small but hitting is the better play.

What about 12 against a 7?

Hit. The dealer is likely to make a pat hand, so standing on 12 gives up.

Which blackjack rules is this answer for?

Six decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double after split allowed, no surrender. The free trainer grades with those exact rules and shows the expected value of each option.
