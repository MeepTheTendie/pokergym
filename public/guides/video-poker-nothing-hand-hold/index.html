Video poker: what to hold with no pair (2026)

Hold two suited high cards if you have them, otherwise a single high card. Discard the rest.

The bottom of the 9/6 Jacks or Better chart is still worth playing. Two suited high cards return about 0.57 per coin, one high card about 0.47, and discarding all five about 0.34. A hand with nothing in it is a small loss, not a dead hand, and the gap between those three numbers is where a lot of long-run return hides.

Suit matters more than rank at the bottom. King-jack suited returns about 0.57 while king-queen unsuited returns about 0.49, because the suited pair can make a royal, a straight flush or a flush as well as a paying pair. That is why the chart puts suited high cards above a higher unsuited pair.

Do not keep three high cards unimproved. The extra cards cut the number of cards you can draw, and the straight chances do not pay enough to make up for it. Two suited high cards, or one, is the fallback.

Questions

What should I hold in video poker with no pair?

Two suited high cards if you have them, otherwise the highest single high card, and discard everything else.

Is it ever right to discard all five cards?

Only when you have no high card and no draw at all. A junk hand with no jack or better returns about 0.34, slightly less than holding one high card at about 0.47.

Why are two suited high cards better than two unsuited?

The suited pair can make a flush, a straight flush and a royal flush as well as a paying pair. Two unsuited high cards can only make a pair or a straight.
