Three of a kind or four to a flush?

The answer

Hold the three of a kind.

Practice five video poker hands →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

Trips pays 3-for-1 as it stands and has two cards to improve into a full house or four of a kind.

Four to a flush completes roughly one time in five. That is a fair draw, but not enough to give up a paying hand for it.

The trainer grades every decision against basic strategy, 9/6 Jacks or Better, or the standard baccarat tableau — the same answer you just read.

Questions

Do you ever break trips?

No. Three of a kind is the strongest non-premium hold in Jacks or Better.

What if the trips are low?

Rank does not matter for trips. Hold them anyway.

Which pay table is this hold for?

Full-pay 9/6 Jacks or Better (9 for a full house, 6 for a flush), which returns about 99.54% with perfect play. The free trainer scores every hold against that table.
