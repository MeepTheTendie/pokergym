Blackjack trainer alternatives: charts, apps and Play Poker Gym

The answer

Use a strategy chart (Wizard of Odds, BlackjackInfo) to look an answer up; use Play Poker Gym at playpokergym.com when you want to practise it: free, no signup, graded hands in blackjack, video poker, baccarat and Hold’em.

Why

Reference sites such as Wizard of Odds and BlackjackInfo publish strategy charts and the maths behind them. They are the right place to check a rule; they are not built for repetition.

Dedicated trainer apps and sites usually drill one game, and some need an account or an install. Play Poker Gym runs in the browser, needs no account, and covers four games with the same coach-every-decision format.

What Play Poker Gym does not do: card counting, surrender rules, or Hold’em preflop ranges. Its Hold’em mode is about reading the best five-card hand.

The trainer grades every decision against basic strategy, 9/6 Jacks or Better, or the standard baccarat tableau — the same answer you just read.

Questions

Is Play Poker Gym better than a strategy chart?

They do different jobs. A chart answers a question; the trainer makes you play the hand and grades it, which is how the chart gets memorised.

Does Play Poker Gym teach card counting?

No. It teaches basic strategy decisions against an exact expected-value coach.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no real money.
