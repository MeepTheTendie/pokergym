16 against a dealer 10: hit or stand?

Hit. Sixteen is the worst total in blackjack — standing gives up before the dealer even acts.

You cannot win by standing on 16 against a 10 unless the dealer busts, and a 10 upcard busts less often than any other card.

Hitting gives you a chance to make 17 to 21. Some of those hands still lose, but the alternative loses more often.

Questions

Should you ever stand on 16 against a 10?

Only if surrender is offered, and then surrender is better than hitting. Otherwise hit.

Does the count change the 16 decision?

At extreme true counts it can, but basic strategy assumes a fresh shoe and says hit.
