How often does Banker win in baccarat? (2026)

About 45.9% of hands, against 44.6% for Player. The remaining 9.5% are ties.

The gap comes from the tableau, not from luck. Player acts first and its draw is mechanical; Banker draws second and its third card depends on what Player did. Seeing Player’s third card is worth about 1.3 percentage points of win rate, and that is the entire reason Banker exists as a separate bet.

After the 5% commission, 45.9% at 0.95 to 1 against 44.6% and 9.5% pushes works out to a 1.06% house edge. That is the cheapest bet on a baccarat table and cheaper than almost anything else in a casino, which is the only strategic decision the game offers.

Runs follow directly from the win rate. Six Banker wins in a row is 0.459 to the sixth power, about 0.9% or 1 in 107. But in 100 hands the longest Banker run you should expect is about five, and a run of eight is unremarkable over 500 hands.

Questions

Does Banker win more often than Player?

Yes, about 45.9% against 44.6%, with 9.5% ties. The 5% commission on Banker is what keeps that advantage from being free money.

What is the longest Banker streak in baccarat?

There is no fixed maximum. Over 100 hands the longest Banker run you should expect is around five; over 500 hands, around seven. Longer runs happen, they are just rarer.

Should you always bet Banker in baccarat?

It is the lowest-edge bet, so yes if you are going to play a negative-expectation game at all. Betting Player costs about 0.18 percentage points more, and Tie costs about 13 points more.
