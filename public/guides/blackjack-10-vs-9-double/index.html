10 against a dealer 9: double or hit?

The answer

Double against a 9. Hit against a 10 or an ace.

Practice five blackjack hands →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

Ten against a 9 is close, and the double is what makes it profitable: you are betting more on a hand that is slightly ahead.

Against a 10 or ace the dealer is too strong, so the same total is a hit rather than a double.

Questions

Do you double 10 against every card?

No. Double against 2 through 9; hit against 10 and ace.

Does the double change if the dealer hits soft 17?

Only at the margins. 10 against 9 stays a double in common rule sets.

Which blackjack rules is this answer for?

Six decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double after split allowed, no surrender. The free trainer grades with those exact rules and shows the expected value of each option.
