10 against a dealer 9: double or hit?

The answer

Double against a 9. Hit against a 10 or an ace.

Practice five blackjack hands →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

Ten against a 9 is close, and the double is what makes it pay: you are betting more on a hand that is slightly ahead. For 6-4 from a full six-deck shoe, the trainer’s engine puts doubling at about +0.15 per unit bet and hitting at about +0.12.

Against a 10 or ace the dealer is too strong, so the same total is a hit rather than a double. Against a 10, hitting is worth about +0.03 and doubling about −0.01.

Rules this answer assumes

Six decks, blackjack pays 3:2, dealer stands on soft 17, double on any first two cards, double after split allowed, split up to four hands, split aces get one card each and can’t be resplit, no surrender, and the dealer checks for blackjack before you act, so a dealer blackjack never takes a double or split bet. These are the rules the free trainer deals and grades with.

What changes the answer

  • Dealer hits or stands on soft 17, four decks or eight: 10 against a 9 is a double, and 10 against a 10 or an ace is a hit.
  • Three or more cards. A 10 like 4-3-3 can’t be doubled in most casinos (the trainer only doubles your first two cards), so hit it.
  • Pair of 5s. 5-5 is a 10, so double it against 2 through 9 and hit it against a 10 or an ace: 5-5 against a 6.
  • After a split. With double after split (the trainer’s rule), a new 10 against a 9 is still a double. Without it, hit.
  • Card counting. With the Hi-Lo count, players also double 10 against a 10 or an ace at high positive counts (a true count of about +4). Basic strategy hits both.

Questions

Do you double 10 against every card?

No. Double against 2 through 9; hit against 10 and ace.

Does the double change if the dealer hits soft 17?

No. In four- to eight-deck games, 10 against a 9 is a double whether the dealer hits or stands on soft 17.

Should you double 10 against a 10?

No. Basic strategy hits 10 against a 10. In the trainer’s six-deck game hitting is worth about +0.03 per unit bet and doubling about −0.01.
