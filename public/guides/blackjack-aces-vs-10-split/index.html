Should you always split aces in blackjack?

The answer

Yes. Always split aces, against every dealer upcard from 2 to ace, including a 10.

Why

Two aces together are a soft 12, one of the worst starting hands. Split, each ace becomes the start of a hand where any ten-value card (about 31% of the shoe) makes 21. That is why aces, along with eights, are the pair basic strategy splits against everything.

The split wins by at least 14 cents per dollar against every card, so there is no exception to learn.

Questions

Do you always split aces in blackjack?

Yes. Aces and eights are the two pairs you split against every dealer upcard in basic strategy. The split is worth at least 14 cents per dollar more than hitting against every card, 2 through ace.

Should you split aces against a dealer ace?

Yes. It is the closest case, but splitting is still about +0.12 of your bet against about −0.02 for hitting.

Is a split ace plus a 10 a blackjack?

No. A 21 made after splitting aces counts as 21, not a natural, so it pays even money rather than 3:2.

What if I can only draw one card to each split ace?

Split anyway. One card per ace is the common rule and the split is still worth roughly a quarter of a bet more than hitting soft 12 against a 10.
