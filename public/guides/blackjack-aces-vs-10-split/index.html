Split aces against a dealer 10? A-A vs 10

The answer

Split. Aces are split against every dealer card, including a 10.

Play this exact hand →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

Two aces together are a soft 12, one of the worst starting hands. Split, each ace becomes the start of a hand where any ten-value card (about 31% of the shoe) makes 21.

Against a 10 the coach rates the split at about +0.18 of your bet and hitting at about −0.07, a swing of a quarter of a bet. You get one card on each split ace in most casinos, and it is still the best play by far.

Questions

Do you always split aces in blackjack?

Yes. Aces and eights are the two pairs you split against every dealer upcard in basic strategy.

Is a split ace plus a 10 a blackjack?

No. A 21 made after splitting aces counts as 21, not a natural, so it pays even money rather than 3:2.

What if I can only draw one card to each split ace?

Split anyway. One card per ace is the common rule and the split is still worth roughly a quarter of a bet more than hitting soft 12 against a 10.
