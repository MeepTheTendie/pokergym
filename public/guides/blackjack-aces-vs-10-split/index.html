Should you always split aces in blackjack?

The answer

Yes. Always split aces, against every dealer upcard from 2 to ace, including a 10.

Why

Two aces together are a soft 12, one of the worst starting hands. Split, each ace becomes the start of a hand where any ten-value card (about 31% of the shoe) makes 21. That is why aces, along with eights, are the pair basic strategy splits against everything.

Per 1 unit bet in the coach’s game (rules below), splitting a pair of aces against a 10 is worth about +0.18 and hitting the soft 12 about −0.07. Against an ace, the closest case, it is +0.12 against −0.02. The split wins by at least 14 cents per dollar against every upcard, so there is no exception to learn. Rules this answer assumes

Six decks, blackjack pays 3:2, dealer stands on soft 17, double on any first two cards, double after split allowed, split up to four hands, split aces get one card each and can’t be resplit, no surrender, and the dealer checks for blackjack before you act, so a dealer blackjack never takes a double or split bet. These are the rules the free trainer deals and grades with.

What changes the answer

  • Dealer hits soft 17. Still split against every upcard. That rule changes a handful of plays, such as 11 against an ace and some soft doubles, but never a pair of aces.
  • Blackjack pays 6:5 instead of 3:2. Still split. A split ace plus a ten is 21, not a blackjack, so it pays even money under either rule. 6:5 makes the whole game worse, not this decision.
  • Resplitting aces allowed. Split, and if another ace lands on a split ace, split again. That only adds value. The trainer does not allow it, so a second ace there is a soft 12 that has to stand, and the numbers above already include that case.
  • One card per split ace. This is the common rule and the one the trainer uses; the table assumes it. Where you may hit or double split aces (rare), the split is worth even more.
  • Number of decks. Still split. Running the same coach engine on a one-, two- or eight-deck shoe gives the same row: split against every upcard.
  • Splitting not allowed (a home game, say). Hit the soft 12. Against a 6 the coach puts doubling and hitting within a fraction of a cent of each other.

Questions

Do you always split aces in blackjack?

Yes. Aces and eights are the two pairs you split against every dealer upcard in basic strategy. The split is worth at least 14 cents per dollar more than hitting against every card, 2 through ace.

Should you split aces against a dealer ace?

Yes. It is the closest case, but splitting is still about +0.12 of your bet against about −0.02 for hitting.

Is a split ace plus a 10 a blackjack?

No. A 21 made after splitting aces counts as 21, not a natural, so it pays even money rather than 3:2.

What if I can only draw one card to each split ace?

Split anyway. One card per ace is the common rule and the split is still worth roughly a quarter of a bet more than hitting soft 12 against a 10.
