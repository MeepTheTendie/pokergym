Pocket pair: how often do you flop a set?

The answer

About 11.8% — roughly once in 8.5 flops.

Practice five Hold’em hands →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

Two of the 50 unseen cards make your set on the flop. The flop is three cards, so the chance at least one of them is yours is 1 − (48/50 × 47/49 × 46/48) ≈ 11.8%.

That number is why small pairs need implied odds. You miss most flops, so the pot has to pay enough when you do hit.

Questions

What are the odds of flopping a set with a pocket pair?

About 11.8%, or roughly 1 in 8.5.

Does a set beat a flush draw?

A set is already a made hand. A flush draw on the flop completes by the river about 35% of the time.

How do I turn outs into odds quickly?

Use the rule of 4 and 2: multiply your outs by 4 on the flop (two cards to come) or by 2 on the turn. Nine flush outs is about 36% on the flop and 18% on the turn.
