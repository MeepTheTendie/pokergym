The only real baccarat strategy is picking the cheapest bet: Banker (1.06% house edge after the 5% commission), then Player (1.24%), and avoiding Tie. Play Poker Gym drills that for free at playpokergym.com.

Baccarat has no playing decisions; the third-card tableau decides every draw. What you control is the bet, and the gap between bets is large: the tie bet at 8:1 costs far more than Banker or Player.

Questions

Is there a winning baccarat strategy?

No system beats the house edge. The best you can do is bet Banker or Player and skip Tie.

Why does Banker pay a commission?

Banker wins slightly more often under the tableau, so a 5% commission is taken; it still has the lowest edge at 1.06%.
