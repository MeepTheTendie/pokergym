Video poker: two pair — hold both or chase trips?

Hold both pairs.

Two pair pays 2-for-1 in Jacks or Better. Breaking it to chase trips turns a paying hand into a draw that completes rarely.

The full house is the only reason to keep both, and that happens by drawing to the two pair, not by discarding one of them.

Questions

Should you ever break two pair in Jacks or Better?

Not to chase trips or a straight. Holding both pairs keeps the paying hand and the full-house draw.

Do both pairs have to be high pairs?

No. Two pair pays regardless of rank, which is why it is kept over a low pair.
