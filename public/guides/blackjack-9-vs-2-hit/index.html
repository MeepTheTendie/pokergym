Blackjack hard 9 vs 2: double or hit? (2026)

Hit. Double hard 9 against 3 through 6, but against a 2 just hit.

A 9 makes 19 with any ten-value card, so doubling is tempting. But a dealer 2 makes a pat hand more often than a 3 through 6, and doubling locks you into one card.

The coach puts hitting at about +0.077 of your bet and doubling at about +0.068. The gap is small, about a cent per dollar, so this is a classic hand people get wrong in both directions. In single-deck games the double sometimes becomes correct.

Questions

Should you double 9 against a 2 in blackjack?

Not in a six-deck game. Hit instead; the double is worth about one cent per dollar less. Some single and double-deck charts double it.

When do you double a hard 9?

Against a dealer 3, 4, 5 or 6. Against 2 and 7 through ace, hit.

Is 9 vs 2 the same with soft 9 (A-8)?

No. Soft 19 (A-8) is a stand against a 2. Only the hard 9 is a hit-or-double question.
