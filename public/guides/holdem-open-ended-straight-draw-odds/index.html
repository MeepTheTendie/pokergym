Open-ended straight draw on the flop: odds?

The answer

About 31.5% by the river with two cards to come.

Practice five Hold’em hands →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

An open-ended draw has eight outs: four cards on each end. With two cards to come, 1 − (39/47 × 38/46) ≈ 31.5%.

On the turn alone it is about 17.4%, because one card of the 46 left completes it eight times.

Questions

How many outs does an open-ended straight draw have?

Eight: four on each end of the straight.

Do you use 4 and 2 to estimate?

Yes. Multiply outs by four on the flop and by two on the turn for a close approximation.

How do I turn outs into odds quickly?

Use the rule of 4 and 2: multiply your outs by 4 on the flop (two cards to come) or by 2 on the turn. Nine flush outs is about 36% on the flop and 18% on the turn.
