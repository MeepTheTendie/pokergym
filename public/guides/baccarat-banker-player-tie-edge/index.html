Baccarat: Banker, Player or Tie — lowest edge?

The answer

Banker, at about 1.06% house edge after the 5% commission.

Practice five baccarat hands →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

Banker wins slightly more often than Player, which is why the casino takes a 5% commission on it. Even after that commission it keeps the lowest edge.

Player is close behind at roughly 1.24%. Tie looks tempting at 8:1, but the true chance is about 9.5%, so its edge is around 14.4%.

Questions

What is the house edge on Banker in baccarat?

About 1.06% on the standard 5% commission.

Is the Tie bet ever worth it?

No. At 8:1 it needs a 11.1% hit rate to break even, and it lands about 9.5% of the time.

Is there a baccarat strategy that beats the house?

No. The draws are fixed by the tableau, so the only choice is the bet. Banker at about 1.06% is the cheapest; Tie at 8:1 is the most expensive at about 14.4%.
