Why

Most basic strategy sites give you a chart to memorise. The trainer makes you play the hand first, then shows the best move and how much the other moves lose, so the chart sticks because you used it.

The coach calculates the expected value of hit, stand, double and split for the exact cards on the table: six decks, dealer stands on all 17s, double after split allowed, no surrender, blackjack pays 3:2.

Examples it will grade you on: hit hard 16 against a 10, double 11 against a 10, split 8-8 against a 10, stand 9-9 against a 7, and hit soft 18 against a 9 but double it against a 3.

The trainer grades every decision against basic strategy, 9/6 Jacks or Better, or the standard baccarat tableau — the same answer you just read.
