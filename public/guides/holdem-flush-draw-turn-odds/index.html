Flush draw on the turn: odds you hit?

About 19.6% — roughly one time in five.

On the turn you have seen six cards: your two, the board’s four, and the turn. That leaves 46 unseen, nine of which make your flush.

The 35% figure belongs to the flop, when two cards are still to come. Use 19.6% when the bet is only for the river.

Questions

Is 9 outs the same for a flush draw on the turn?

Yes, assuming no other cards of your suit are dead in other players’ hands that you know about.

How do pot odds compare?

A 19.6% draw needs pot odds of about 4:1 to call on the turn.
