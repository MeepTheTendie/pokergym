Baccarat: Player has 5 — does Player draw?

The answer

Yes. Player draws on 0–5 and stands on 6–7.

Play five baccarat hands →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

The Player hand is mechanical: the first two cards decide it, with no judgement and no optional draw. A total of 5 is always a draw.

Banker is the hand with conditions. Banker draws on 0–5 unless the Player’s third card changes the rule, which is why the printed tableau exists.

Questions

Does Player ever stand on 5 in baccarat?

No. Player stands only on 6 and 7. Every total from 0 to 5 draws.

Is the Player bet better than Banker?

No. Banker has the lower house edge, about 1.06% against 1.24% for Player, before commission.

Is there a baccarat strategy that beats the house?

No. The draws are fixed by the tableau, so the only choice is the bet. Banker at about 1.06% is the cheapest; Tie at 8:1 is the most expensive at about 14.4%.
