Jacks or Better: pair of jacks or 4-card flush?

Hold the pair of jacks.

A high pair is a made paying hand. Four to a flush is only a draw: it completes about once in five attempts (9 of the 47 unseen cards).

That is the opposite of the low-pair decision. With a low pair against four to a flush, the flush draw is worth more because the pair pays nothing on its own.

Questions

Does a pair of jacks beat a flush draw in Jacks or Better?

Yes. The high pair already pays even money; four to a flush misses about 80% of the time.

What about a low pair against four to a flush?

Then keep the flush draw. A low pair pays nothing without improvement, and the draw is the stronger hold.
