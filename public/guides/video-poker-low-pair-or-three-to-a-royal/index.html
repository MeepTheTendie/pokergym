Jacks or Better: low pair or 3 to a royal? (2026)

Hold the three to a royal and throw the low pair away.

A low pair pays nothing until it improves to two pair or better. Three suited royal cards like K-Q-J can still make a royal (800 for 1 at max coins), a straight flush, a flush, a straight, or a paying high pair.

In the exact deal 5-5 with K-Q-J suited, the coach rates holding K-Q-J at about 1.02 per coin bet and holding 5-5 at about 0.82. That is one of the few holds where breaking a pair is right.

Questions

Do you break a low pair for 3 to a royal?

Yes, in 9/6 Jacks or Better. Three to a royal ranks above a low pair; four to a royal ranks above even a high pair.

Do you break a high pair (jacks or better) for 3 to a royal?

No. A paying high pair beats three to a royal. Only four to a royal beats a high pair.

What about a low pair against 4 to a flush?

Hold the four to a flush. It returns more than a low pair in 9/6 Jacks or Better.
