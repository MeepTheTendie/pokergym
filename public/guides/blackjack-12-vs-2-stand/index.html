12 against a dealer 2: hit or stand?

Stand. Against a 2 or a 3, let the dealer take the risk.

A dealer 2 or 3 is the weakest upcard. The dealer has to draw to 17 and will bust more often than from a 7 through ace.

Hitting 12 risks busting on any ten, and you already lose most of the hands where you bust. Standing keeps the dealer honest.

Questions

Is 12 against a 2 really a stand?

Yes. Basic strategy stands on 12 against 2 and 3, and hits against 4 through 6 only in some rule sets — the safe rule is stand on 12 against 2 or 3.

What about 12 against a 7?

Hit. The dealer is likely to make a pat hand, so standing on 12 gives up.
