AK against a pocket pair: who is ahead?

The pocket pair is ahead, roughly 55–57% against 43–45%.

The pair already has a made hand. AK has to improve, but it has six outs to a pair and two cards to come plus the board’s help.

The closer the pair is to the aces and kings in your hand, the less room AK has. QQ against AK is worse for the ace-king than 22 is.

Questions

Is AK a coin flip against a pair?

Roughly. The pair is about 55–57%, so it is close but not even.

Why does the pair’s rank matter?

Because a pair of queens or kings blocks some of the cards AK needs, while low pairs leave more outs.
