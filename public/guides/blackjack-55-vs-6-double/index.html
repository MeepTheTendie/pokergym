5-5 against a 6: split or double?

Double. Never split fives — 5-5 is a hard 10, not a pair worth splitting.

Splitting fives turns a strong 10 into two hands starting on 5, each of which needs two good cards to become anything.

Against a 6 the dealer is likely to bust, so doubling the 10 puts more money on the best hand at the table.

Questions

Do you ever split fives?

No. Treat 5-5 as a hard 10 and use the hard-total strategy.

What about 5-5 against a 10?

Double 10 against 2–9; against a 10 the same total is a hit.
