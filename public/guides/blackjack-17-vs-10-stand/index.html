17 against a dealer 10: hit or stand?

The answer

Stand. Seventeen against a 10 is always a stand in basic strategy.

Why

A dealer 10 finishes with 17 or better about 79% of the time, so a 17 that stands wins only on the 21% dealer bust and pushes on the 11% of hands that finish on 17. That still adds up to losing about 42 cents per dollar, which is better than throwing the hand away.

Hitting is much worse because the bust risk is enormous. A 17 busts on every rank from five upward — nine of the thirteen ranks, about 73% of the cards left — so hitting trades a hand that sometimes wins for one that usually dies immediately. The coach rates hit at about −0.58 against −0.42 for standing.

The card matters less than players expect. Standing on 17 against a 7 loses only about 11 cents, and against an ace about 48 cents, but it is a stand against all of them. There is no dealer upcard where hitting a hard 17 is correct.

Questions

Should you ever hit 17 in blackjack?

Not a hard 17. Soft 17 is a different hand and is always a hit or a double, never a stand. Hard 17 stands against every dealer card.

Why do people hit 17 against a 10?

Because standing feels like giving up. Over a long run it is not: you keep the 21% of hands where the dealer busts, and hitting busts you before the dealer acts about 73% of the time.

Is 17 vs 10 a surrender hand?

Yes, in rule sets that offer late surrender on the first two cards, 17 against a 10 is a surrender. This trainer uses rules with no surrender, so the answer here is stand.
