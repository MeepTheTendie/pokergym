Baccarat: lowest house edge still means expected loss.

Choose an answer before opening each explanation. These questions use Poker Gym’s fresh eight-deck shoe, standard drawing rules, 5% commission on Banker wins and an 8:1 Tie payout. Player and Banker wagers push on a tie. Different payouts or rules change the math.

1. Which side loses the least on average?

Compare Banker, Player and Tie under those rules. Which has the lowest house edge?

Reveal the answer and reason

Banker. The simulator calculates expected losses of about 1.06% for Banker, 1.24% for Player and 14.36% for Tie per unit staked. Banker’s edge already includes the commission. All three have negative expected value; “best of these choices” does not mean profitable.

2. What does a 5% commission leave?

In a hypothetical example, a 20-unit Banker wager wins. How many units of profit remain after commission, excluding the returned stake: 19, 20 or 21?

Reveal the answer and reason

19 units. Commission is 20 × 0.05 = 1 unit. Net winnings are 20 × 0.95 = 19 units. The returned original stake is separate: the total returned would be 39 units. A winning round does not remove the long-run expected loss.

3. Is an 8:1 Tie payout fair?

The simulator’s fresh-shoe Tie probability is about 9.52%. An 8:1 payout means eight units of profit plus the returned stake when it wins. Does that payout break even on average?

Reveal the answer and reason

No. Expected value is probability of winning × 8 minus probability of losing × 1. Using the unrounded probability, that is about −0.1436 units per unit staked, or a 14.36% expected loss. The break-even profit payout would be about 9.5:1. A large advertised payout alone does not make a wager favorable.

Practice the reasoning, not a betting system

Baccarat’s draw rules determine the cards; players do not choose hit or stand. Poker Gym tests your understanding of odds and payouts. It does not teach a streak-prediction method. These full-shoe averages do not predict the next hand or promise a result in a short session.
