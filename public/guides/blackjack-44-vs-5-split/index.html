Should you split 4s against a dealer 5?

The answer

Split if you can double after splitting. If you cannot, hit.

Play this exact hand →The coach grades your call and explains it. Free, no signup.

Why

A pair of fours is a hard 8: you never double it and you never stand. The only question is whether two hands starting with a 4 beat one hand of 8.

A 5 is one of the dealer’s weakest cards. With double after split, each 4 that catches a 6 or 7 becomes a 10 or 11 you can double, so the coach rates splitting at about +0.11 of your bet against +0.08 for hitting. Take away the double after split and that edge disappears, so hit.

Questions

Do you split 4s against a 4?

Only against 5 and 6, and only with double after split. Against a 4 and every other card, hit the hard 8.

Should you double 4-4 as a hard 8?

No. Hard 8 is never a double in six-deck basic strategy; the hand is too far from 21.

When should you split 4s in blackjack?

Against a dealer 5 or 6 when the table lets you double after splitting. Otherwise never split them.
